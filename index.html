About this map

By day, Jan designs fintech and insurance products at The Monday Club (themondayclub.studio).

Walking the streets of Barcelona, he makes a note of places. He has kept a list since 2021, never meant as a project, just something to jog his own memory.

No stars, no rankings, no paid placements. Only places he has been to himself, with people who matter to him. His Google Maps is covered in pins, because nothing comes closer to plain contentment for him than a good spot, good food and the right people at the table.

This list is not a guide. It is a collection of moments that were worth a second time.
